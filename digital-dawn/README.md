# DIGITAL DAWN — Pixelia Chronicles

A 16-bit-style monster-tamer RPG in the spirit of Digimon World 3, Cyber Sleuth and Time Stranger, with an **original story**.
Open `index.html` in a browser (no build, no assets, no dependencies).

Every graphic is raw pixels: sprites are hand-placed character masks compiled to canvases (auto-outlined and shaded), tiles and
backgrounds are drawn pixel by pixel, the font is a 5x7 bitmap, and music/SFX are synthesized chiptune (WebAudio).

**Controls:** Arrows/WASD move · Z/Enter confirm/talk · X/Esc menu/cancel · M mute · swipe/tap on touch screens.

**Story:** On the last night of *Dawnbit Online*, Kai is pulled into Pixelia with partner Embermon. Gather Aquabit and Sprigmon,
win the Dawn Crest from the Glitchwraith, and climb the Null Tower to face the Null Sovereign — a forgotten partner.

**Features:** overworld + 4 areas, random encounters, 1–3 v 1–3 turn-based battles with elemental weaknesses, 9 VFX types
(fire, claw, slash, bolt, water, tidal wave, leaf, void glitch, heal), level-ups, Embermon → Blazewyrm evolution cutscene,
inn/shop/save, two-phase final boss, ending credits.
